Privacy policy

Effective 1 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Data Clerk Android app ("Data Clerk", "the app") handles, where it is kept and how you can remove it.

Summary

  • The datasets and records you create are stored only on your device.
  • An account is optional. If you create one, we store your email address and a secure password hash so you can sign in.
  • Google Drive is optional. If you connect it, the app can only create and access its own files in your Drive.
  • We do not sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

Information the app handles

InformationWhere it is keptWhy
Datasets and records you enterOn your device onlySo you can view, edit and export your data
Email address and password (stored as a secure hash)Our sign-in provider, SupabaseTo create your account and let you sign in, confirm your email and reset your password
Google account email and Drive access tokenOn your device onlyTo show which Google account is connected and to upload files you choose to save to Drive
App settings, such as light or dark modeOn your device onlyTo remember your preferences

Your datasets are never uploaded to our servers. They leave your device only when you export a file yourself or save a copy to your own Google Drive.

Google user data

When you choose to connect Google Drive, Data Clerk asks Google for these permissions:

  • Basic profile and email address, to identify which Google account is connected.
  • Google Drive: files created by this app only (drive.file), to save CSV copies of datasets you choose to back up.

Data Clerk uses this access only to upload the files you ask it to save. It cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive. Google data is not sent to our servers, not shared with anyone else, not used for advertising and not used to train AI or machine learning models. People do not read your Google data.

Data Clerk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information. The only outside services involved are:

  • Supabase, which runs account sign-in and sends confirmation and password-reset emails.
  • Google, only if you connect Google Drive, to sign you in and store the files you save.

We may disclose information if required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Datasets: delete any dataset in the app. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on the device.
  • Google Drive: disconnect in the app's Settings. You can also remove Data Clerk's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already saved to your Drive stay there until you delete them.
  • Account: email kelvinmatita6@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Security

Connections to Supabase and Google use encrypted HTTPS. Passwords are never stored in plain text. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Data Clerk is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
