AgriSkill AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

AgriSkill AI is a learning platform for agriculture students, graduates and farmers. This page explains what information the website and Android app use, and why.

Without an account

You can use courses, quizzes and the Project Lab without an account. Your name, learning plan and progress are then saved only on your own device (in the browser or app storage). We do not receive them.

With a free account

  • Email address and password — to sign you in. Passwords are stored securely (hashed) by our authentication provider; we cannot see them.
  • Learning profile — the name you choose, country, background, goals, level and study time, used to personalise your learning path.
  • Learning progress — lessons completed, quiz scores, Project Lab answers and notes, and portfolio entries, so you can continue on any device.
  • AI Tutor usage count — how many tutor questions you asked each day, to apply a fair daily limit.

This data is stored with Supabase in the European Union (Ireland). Security rules ensure each learner can only access their own records.

AI Tutor

When you ask the AI Tutor a question, your message, recent conversation and any photo you attach are sent through our server to Anthropic, whose Claude model writes the answer. Your country, background, level and goals are included so the answer suits you. We do not save tutor conversations on our servers; the conversation is kept only on your device until you close the app or start a new chat. Please don't include personal details about other people in your questions or photos.

The AI Tutor is a learning aid and can make mistakes. Always confirm diagnoses, chemical or drug doses and regulations with a qualified expert.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • We don't use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

Your choices

  • Delete your progress: in the app, go to Profile → Reset everything. When signed in, this deletes your progress from the cloud as well as your device.
  • Delete your account: email kelvinmatita6@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and we will delete your account within 30 days.
  • Correct your details: edit your learning plan in Profile at any time.

Children

AgriSkill AI is intended for learners aged 16 and over. Younger learners should use it with a parent, guardian or teacher.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
